Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Clarity AI helps you rehearse conversations such as interviews, negotiations, presentations and pitches. We collect as little as we can, and we never sell your data.

What happens to your voice

  • Chrome, Edge and Android: live captions use your browser's built-in speech recognition, which is provided by your browser vendor (for example Google for Chrome) under their terms.
  • Other browsers: your recording is sent once to our server and forwarded to a speech-to-text provider (open-source Whisper, hosted by Groq) to produce a transcript. We don't store the audio.
  • Spoken replies may be generated by ElevenLabs from the coach's text. Your voice is never sent to ElevenLabs.

What happens to your camera

The camera coach is optional and off by default. When you turn it on, body-language analysis (eye contact, posture, gestures) runs entirely on your device using Google's open-source MediaPipe. Video is never uploaded. Only summary numbers, such as "eye contact 64%", are sent with your practice so the AI can include them in your feedback.

Transcripts and feedback

To review your practice, the transcript and delivery numbers are sent to an AI model provider (for example Groq or Anthropic). Providers process this to produce your feedback; we don't use it to train models. As a guest, your reports are kept only in your browser tab. If you're signed in, AI-reviewed reports are saved privately to your account so you can see your progress.

Accounts

  • Username accounts: we store your username and a salted, one-way hash of your password. We can't read your password.
  • Google sign-in: we receive your name, email address, profile picture and a Google account ID. We use them only to sign you in and personalise the app.
  • Sessions use a secure, HttpOnly cookie. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

Leaderboard

Scores are private unless you choose to publish one, under a public name you pick. You can remove published scores at any time.

Anonymous usage counts

We count events such as "a practice was completed" to understand usage. These counts contain no transcripts, names or emails; visitors are identified only by a salted hash of a random browser ID.

Your choices and rights

You can delete your account and all saved reports at any time from Progress → Delete my account and data. Depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR or India's DPDP Act), you may also have rights to access, correct or export your data. Contact us to exercise them.
