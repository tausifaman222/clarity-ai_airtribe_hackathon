---
name: Communication confidence positioning
description: Product mission and how startup pitching fits into it
---

Present the product as helping people speak confidently and put their ideas across clearly to anyone. Startup pitching is one use case, not the whole product's mission.

For the initial audience, focus the first practice journey and feedback on early-stage founders and builders. Don't assume they have mature metrics or a finished product.

**Why:** The user explicitly described communication confidence as the mission and named early-stage founders/builders as the initial audience.

**How to apply:** Lead with communication confidence while making the first startup-pitch journey relevant to early founders. Treat interviews, observations and experiments as valid early evidence, label assumptions honestly, and clearly distinguish available practice from future scenarios.
