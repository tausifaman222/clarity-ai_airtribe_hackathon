---
name: Communication confidence positioning
description: Product mission and how startup pitching fits into it
---

Present the product as helping people speak confidently and put their ideas across clearly to anyone. Startup pitching is one use case, not the whole product's mission.

**Why:** The user explicitly clarified this while requesting a better design and user journey.

**How to apply:** Lead with communication confidence in naming, home-screen messaging, and onboarding. Clearly distinguish currently available practice from future scenarios; don't imply those scenarios already work.
