**Rule:** Use one practice path rather than asking founders to choose between separate Coach and live-interview modes. Show every stage checklist point up front, offer a short guided warm-up, then an explicitly started, uninterrupted five-minute investor interview, followed by the detailed improvement report.

**Why:** The user chose this sequence and asked that the full checklist be visible before the interview; early-stage founders need to know what they're practising before the timer begins.

**How to apply:** Keep Coach inside the warm-up-to-interview journey, keep the timer and interviewer silent during the pitch, and reserve detailed evaluation for the report afterward.
